# Workout keyboard controls

On Train, press **Enter** to record the next set and start rest. While rest is running, Enter pauses it; another Enter resumes it. **Left arrow** and **Right arrow** move to the previous or next exercise without changing recorded sets or rest timers. Navigation stops at either end of the workout.

Shortcuts leave number fields, selects, editable text, dialogs and other app views alone. Enter on a focused link, disclosure or another button keeps its normal action. Held keys do not repeat workout shortcuts, and modified keys keep their browser behavior. Tab still moves through the normal controls. Keyboard hints appear on devices with a mouse or trackpad, and the action/navigation buttons expose their shortcuts to assistive technology.

Both exercise navigation cards show the neighboring exercise's current setup angle, including 0°, or “Angle not set.” This comes from the workout snapshot so historical angle settings remain accurate.
